Delete your account

You can delete your PitchPerfect Tuner account directly in the app. You must be signed in to complete the process.

On this page

  1. How to delete your account
  2. What will be deleted
  3. Before you continue

01 How to delete your account

To request deletion from the app:

  1. Open PitchPerfect Tuner and sign in to your account.
  2. Open the Account Details screen.
  3. Choose Delete Account and follow the on-screen instructions to confirm.

Account deletion is completed through the app while you are signed in; it cannot be requested from this webpage.

02 What will be deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes your user information, including your name, email address, and password, as well as your app data stored in the cloud.

03 Before you continue

Account deletion is permanent. Once confirmed, your account and the associated information described above cannot be restored.
